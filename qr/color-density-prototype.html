RGB-layered QR reliability trial

Question: Can three ordinary QR matrices, carried in the red, green, and blue channels of one image, survive clean PNG, resizing, JPEG loss, and a phone photo while reconstructing the original bytes exactly?

This is an isolated experiment, not a proposed product UI. Each layer has a small frame and CRC-32 checksum. All three must decode before the trial passes.
