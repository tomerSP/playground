About

I’m a junior programmer from Israel. I have a bachelor’s in computer science, and I work at Samsung’s R&D center here.

For a long time I had a list of project ideas I never dared to start. Using AI is what finally got me to take the first step, so I’m starting small. This site is where those small things end up.

There’s nothing like the feeling of “I made it work! I figured it out!” A part of me is disappointed I didn’t actually do it myself. A different part of me is happy for the chance to aim higher. I’ll see where those take me.
